## 🎮 What is Class of '09?

*Class of '09* (and its sequel, *Class of '09: The Re-Up*) is a critically acclaimed narrative comedy visual novel set in an American high school during the late 2000s. 

Unlike traditional visual novels where you read silent text blocks, *Class of '09* plays like an **interactive audio drama**:
* **100% Full Professional Voice Acting:** Every single character (Nicole, Jecka, Emily, Kelly, Jeffrey, Coach Colby, Mr. White, etc.) and every line of dialogue is completely voiced by top-tier voice actors.
* **Branching Storylines:** Packed with dozens of high-stakes social choices, resulting in over 15 unique, wildly chaotic endings.
* **Ultra-Low PC Specs:** Built on Ren'Py, meaning it runs smoothly at 60 FPS on any budget PC, older laptop, or integrated graphics chip without lagging.

## 🔊 What Does This Mod Do?

While the voice acting makes the story great to listen to, blind and visually impaired players previously could not navigate the silent image menus or know what choices they were picking. 

**Class of '09 Access** bridges every visual element directly to your screen reader:

* **Direct NVDA & Tolk Client:** Communicates instantaneously with **NVDA** through `nvdaControllerClient`, with native fallback to **Tolk** (JAWS / SuperNova) and **Windows SAPI5**.
* **Spoken Branching Decisions:** Automatically announces choice menus and options. Arrow keys step through choices with spoken feedback (e.g. *"Choice 1 of 2: HUMOR THE SCHOOL TOUR"*), and pressing **`C`** repeats choices anytime.
* **Live Dialogue & Character Names:** Speaks speaker names and text in real-time. Press **`H`** to repeat the last line, or **`D`** to toggle dialogue TTS (voice acting only vs voice + speech).
* **Accessible Menus & Save Slots:** Silent graphical buttons on the main menu are replaced with accessible buttons and numeric shortcuts (`1`–`5`). Save and load slots announce date/time and empty status.

## 🚀 Installation Instructions

1. Download **`Class09-Access-v1.0.1.zip`** from the Assets below.
2. Extract the archive into your game's **`game/`** subfolder:
   * **Steam:** `Steam\steamapps\common\Class of '09\game\`
   * **The Re-Up:** `Steam\steamapps\common\Class of '09 - The Re-Up\game\`
3. Launch `Class_Of_09.exe`. You will immediately hear NVDA announce the Main Menu!
